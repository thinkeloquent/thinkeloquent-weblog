Topic 03

Agentic Payments & Cryptographic Trust Layer

Replacing synchronous human CVV authentication with delegated intent mandates and machine-to-machine rails.

⚠️ What (Problem Statement)

Traditional payment rails rely on synchronous human interactive scans or passwords—infeasible for background agent purchases. Financial institutions anticipate a 78% surge in agentic fraud. Card networks face broken liability chains if an agent purchases beyond user intent, creating massive chargeback exposure across fragmented protocols (Visa TAP, Mastercard Agent Pay, Stripe MPP, x402, AP2).

🔒 Cryptographic Mandate Architecture (AP2 Standard)

1️⃣
Intent Mandate: Signed by user's wallet with explicit spend caps and category restrictions.
2️⃣
Cart Mandate: Signed by merchant, cryptographically binding specific SKUs, taxes, and prices.
3️⃣
Payment Mandate: Network links funding source to the matched Intent + Cart hash.
